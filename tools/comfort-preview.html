Matched bot continuations

Two fixed seeds. Each continuation begins from the same first-charter checkpoint for its seed, without grants. We compare standard staffing with an existing option that gives comfort services earlier staffing priority.

These are diagnostic cases, not a win-rate estimate or a verdict on human play. Events, festivals and choices can diverge after the curves change. “Cutoff” means the colony was still playing at the observation limit.

Both candidates had higher minimum moods and no settler losses in these continuations. Completion times were mixed: some improved, some slowed, and some remained unfinished at the cutoff.

Prototype values remain separate from the game’s default balance. No new goods or buildings are required.
